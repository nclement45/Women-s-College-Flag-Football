Women's College Flag Football is Booming in New York

As the sport continues to rapidly expand, New York is an emerging hub for the future of women’s college flag football.

By Nia Clement
Nov. 25, 2026

Among the 10 states with the most women's college flag football programs, New York ranks second, with 37 programs, behind California with 47.

In New York, support from NFL teams fueled growth at the highschool level, and the momentum is carrying into college. “There are a lot of potential players who want to play in flag college flag football in college, where that opportunity just didn't exist five years ago with more than 10 teams,” said Matthew Dixon, founder of Collegiate Flag Football.

Professor Ryan Creps at the University of Buffalo said, “our current research finds some evidence that college coaches are recruiting both HS FF student athletes and athletes from other sports within their college/universities to join the FF team even if the athletes have never played the sport before.” The NCAA looking to vote on adding it as a championship sport this January, according to Dixon.
